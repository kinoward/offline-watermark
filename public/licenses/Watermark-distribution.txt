Watermark

Watermark uses ImageMagick WebAssembly with statically linked libraries.
These include libde265, GLib, libheif, liblqr and LibRaw, which are available
under the GNU Lesser General Public License. Their copyright notices and
license terms are in ImageMagick-NOTICE.txt below. You may modify those
libraries and reverse engineer the combined work to debug those changes.

The corresponding source and rebuild instructions are available for the
release listed above, alongside the complete Watermark application source.
